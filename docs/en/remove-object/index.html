Remove Objects & People from Photos

Paint over photobombers, wires or clutter — AI erases them seamlessly.

🔒 Files never leave your device

What this tool does

Brush over unwanted people, objects or clutter, and AI repaints that spot to blend in with the surroundings. It's great for photobombers in travel shots, power lines across the sky, or litter and stains on the ground. After each erase you can paint over leftovers and erase again, and Undo takes you back a step. The AI model runs inside your browser, so your photo is never uploaded.

How to use

  1. Upload your photo.
  2. Paint over what you want to remove. Adjust the brush size, and use Undo if you slip.
  3. Click 'Erase with AI' and save. If anything is left, paint over it and erase again.

FAQ

Is my photo uploaded?

No. The AI runs inside your browser, so your photo is never sent anywhere.

Why is the first run slow?

The first time, your browser downloads the AI model (about 28 MB) and its runtime (about 6 MB). After that they're stored in your browser and start right away.

How long does it take?

Once the model is loaded, each erase usually takes a few seconds. On our PC (Chrome) it took 0.2–0.8 seconds with graphics acceleration (WebGPU) and 2–2.5 seconds without it (CPU). It may be slower on your device.

The erased area looks smudged.

The AI doesn't know what was really behind the object — it paints in something plausible from the surroundings. Small objects on simple backgrounds like sky, sea, grass or walls look natural, but very large areas or detailed spots like text and faces can look blurry or odd. Try erasing in several smaller passes.

Tips

  • Paint a little beyond the object and include its shadow to avoid leftover outlines.
  • For large objects, erasing piece by piece often looks cleaner than doing it all at once.
  • Photos larger than 4096px on the long side are reduced to 4096px for editing and saved at that size.
