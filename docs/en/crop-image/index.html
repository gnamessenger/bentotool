Crop Images

Perfect ratios for Instagram and YouTube. Rotate and flip too.

🔒 Files never leave your device

How to use

  1. Upload the photo you want to crop.
  2. Pick a ratio and drag the frame over the area you want.
  3. Click 'Crop & save' to download.

FAQ

Which ratio should I use for Instagram?

Feed posts: 1:1 (square) or 4:5 (portrait). Reels and Stories: 9:16.

Will the quality drop?

No. The cropped area keeps the original resolution.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser.
