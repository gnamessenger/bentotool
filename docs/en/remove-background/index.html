FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No limits and no watermarks.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Everything runs inside your browser and your photo is never sent anywhere.

Why is the first run slow?

The first time, your browser downloads the AI model (about 40 MB). After that it's instant.

What photos work best?

Photos with a clear subject — people, products, animals — give the cleanest results.
