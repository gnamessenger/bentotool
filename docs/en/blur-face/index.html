Blur Faces in Photos

AI finds faces and hides them automatically. Add license plates or name tags by hand.

🔒 Files never leave your device

How to use

  1. Upload a photo — AI finds faces and blurs them automatically.
  2. Drag on the photo to add areas, or remove boxes with ✕.
  3. Choose pixelate or blur, set strength, and save.

FAQ

What if a face isn't detected?

Just drag on the photo to draw a box over it. Works for license plates, name tags and addresses too.

Is my photo sent to a server?

No. Face detection runs inside your browser, so it's safe for sensitive photos.

Can the blur be undone from the saved image?

No. The pixels are permanently changed in the saved file.
