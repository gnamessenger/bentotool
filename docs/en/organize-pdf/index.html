Organize PDF Pages

Rotate, remove and rearrange pages while you see them.

🔒 Files never leave your device

What this tool does

This tool lays out every page of your PDF as a thumbnail so you can rotate, remove and reorder pages while you look at them. Use it to fix sideways scans, drop blank or unwanted pages, or sort a jumbled document. Pages are copied as they are rather than turned into images, so text stays selectable and quality is unchanged. Everything happens inside your browser.

How to use

  1. Add a PDF — every page appears as a small thumbnail.
  2. Rotate with ↻, remove with ✕, and drag cards to reorder (◀ ▶ buttons on phones).
  3. Click 'Save PDF' to download the edited file.

FAQ

Can I fix sideways scanned pages?

Yes. Click ↻ on that page until it's upright. There are buttons to rotate every page at once, too.

Does saving change text or quality?

No. Pages are copied as they are — not turned into images — so text stays selectable and quality is unchanged.

Is it safe for sensitive documents?

Yes. Files are never sent to a server.

Does it work with very long PDFs?

Yes. Thumbnails are drawn small, one at a time, starting with the pages on screen, to keep things light. Very large files may still take a while to open and save depending on your device's memory.

Tips

  • Removed a page by mistake? 'Start over' brings every page back (and resets rotation and order).
  • The 'orig. p.n' label under each card shows where the page was in the original file.
  • To pull out just a few pages use 'Split PDF'; to combine several PDFs use 'Merge PDF'.
