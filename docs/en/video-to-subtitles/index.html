Auto-Generate Subtitles from Video

Speech to text — SRT, VTT and a transcript in one go.

🔒 Files never leave your device

What this tool does

This tool uses AI (OpenAI's open Whisper model) to transcribe the speech in a video or audio file into timed subtitles (SRT, VTT) and plain text (TXT). Use it for YouTube or Reels captions, lecture and meeting transcripts, or call recordings. You can preview the subtitles with your video and fix any words or timings before saving. The AI runs inside your browser, so your video and audio are never uploaded.

How to use

  1. Choose the language and model (Fast or Accurate).
  2. Add a video or audio file — AI starts transcribing.
  3. Preview the subtitles, fix any mistakes, and save as SRT, VTT or TXT.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No sign-up and no limits.

Is my video uploaded?

No. The AI runs inside your browser, so your video and audio are never sent anywhere. Only the AI program files are downloaded from a public repository the first time.

Why is the first run slow?

The first time, your browser downloads the AI model: on a regular computer (CPU) about 80 MB for Fast and 240 MB for Accurate. Browsers that can use your graphics card run much faster but use somewhat bigger files (Fast about 160–200 MB, Accurate about 290 MB, Most accurate about 540 MB). After that it isn't downloaded again in the same browser.

How accurate is it?

It depends on the model and the audio. Clear English speech usually comes out well even with Fast. For Korean we measured on 15 public speech clips (about 2 min 40 s): about 15% of characters were wrong with Fast, about 7% with Accurate and about 3% with Most accurate. Those were clearly read sentences; real videos with noise or fast speech will have more errors. Names, jargon and numbers are the most common mistakes, so skim the text before saving.

Does it work with long videos?

Yes, but speed varies a lot between computers. On our test PC an 11-minute video took about 1.5 minutes with Accurate on the graphics card, and about 4 minutes with Fast on the CPU alone. Accurate on the CPU can take about as long as the video itself. The whole soundtrack is held in memory, so videos over an hour or files over 1 GB may make the browser stall or fail — extract the audio with 'Video to MP3' first or split the video.

Tips

  • Clear English speech came out well even with Fast in our tests. For other languages such as Korean, the smaller model makes noticeably more mistakes — use Accurate or Most accurate.
  • Loud background music or people talking over each other lowers accuracy. If an odd line like 'Thanks for watching' appears at the end or in a silent part, delete it with ✕.
  • Timings can be off by about a second. Play a line with ▶ and adjust the time boxes. SRT files can be uploaded directly to YouTube, CapCut or Premiere.
